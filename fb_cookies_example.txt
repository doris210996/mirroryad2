# Facebook Authentication Setup

## How to get Facebook cookies:

1. **Login to Facebook** in your browser
2. **Open Developer Tools** (F12)
3. **Go to Application/Storage → Cookies**
4. **Copy these cookies:**
   - c_user
   - xs
   - datr (optional)

5. **Save them in a file** called `fb_cookies.json`:

{
  "c_user": "YOUR_USER_ID",
  "xs": "YOUR_XS_COOKIE",
  "datr": "YOUR_DATR_COOKIE"
}

6. **Update server.py** to load cookies before scraping

## Alternative: Use environment variables
export FB_COOKIE_C_USER="your_c_user_value"
export FB_COOKIE_XS="your_xs_value"
